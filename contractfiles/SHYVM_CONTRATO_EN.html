GLOSSARY / DEFINITIONS

For purposes of this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below, whether used in singular or plural:

1. WORK (Composition): The musical work in its authorial sense (music and/or lyrics), independent of any recording.

2. PHONOGRAM / MASTER: The sound fixation of a performance/execution of a WORK — i.e., a recording, including versions, mixes and final master, capable of commercial exploitation.

3. ONE-STOP: A situation in which THE LABEL/PUBLISHER has sufficient authority to grant, on its own and according to the type of license: (i) 100% of authorizations over the WORK (composition) for synchronization; and/or (ii) 100% of authorizations over the PHONOGRAM (master) for Master Use; and, when offered as "full ONE-STOP" (synchronization + Master Use), both at 100% — without requiring additional third-party authorizations.

4. RIGHTS CLEARANCE: The process of verifying and obtaining authorizations and documentation necessary to exploit or license a WORK and/or PHONOGRAM (holders, percentages, samples/third-party material, chain of title and supporting documentation).

5. SYNCHRONIZATION: A license/authorization to fix or incorporate a WORK in conjunction with images or audiovisual content (films, series, commercials, videogames, social media, etc.).

6. Phonogram Use (Master Use): A license/authorization to use a specific PHONOGRAM in audiovisual content or a specific use, independent of the synchronization license for the WORK.

7. ANNEX 1 — SONG PERCENTAGE SHEET: A per-song form identifying the WORK and/or PHONOGRAM and establishing, confirming, and signing the applicable ownership percentages — forming an integral part of this Agreement.

8. Authorship Percentage: Ownership percentages in the WORK (composition) attributable to authors and/or holders/publishers, which must collectively total 100% of the composition.

9. Phonogram Percentage (Master): Ownership percentages applicable to the PHONOGRAM (master), which must collectively total 100% of the PHONOGRAM.

10. Works/Phonograms List: Consolidated list (Annex 1A) that may enumerate WORKS/PHONOGRAMS and metadata; in case of discrepancy, the signed ANNEX 1 for the relevant song prevails.

11. EDITORIAL ADMINISTRATION: The powers to manage, register, administer and collect patrimonial royalties for the WORK with collective management organizations and/or payers, for the agreed term, without implying assignment of moral rights.

12. EDITORIAL CONTROL FOR SYNCHRONIZATION: Authority to approve, negotiate and formalize synchronization licenses for the WORK, and to conduct the corresponding RIGHTS CLEARANCE process.

13. ANNEX 2 — THIRD-PARTY LICENSES: Annex where licenses and written permissions for SAMPLES and/or third-party material are declared and/or attached, when applicable.

14. ANNEX 3 — PERMANENT ADHESION AND ONE-STOP AUTHORIZATION: Document by which a third-party rights holder adheres to the Main Agreement and authorizes THE LABEL/PUBLISHER to license their participation per the corresponding ANNEX 1.

15. ADHERING PARTY: Any third party who signs ANNEX 3, bound to this Agreement only regarding their own participation in the WORKS/PHONOGRAMS where they sign the corresponding ANNEX 1, per Clause 24 BIS.

16. COVERED WORKS: Those WORKS and/or PHONOGRAMS for which an ADHERING PARTY has signed ANNEX 1, activating that party's authorizations per their ANNEX 3.

17. SAMPLES / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL: Any use of pre-existing recordings, third-party compositions, interpolations or material owned by a third party that requires written authorization/license for exploitation; documentation must be attached in ANNEX 2 when applicable.

In case of interpretive doubt, the terms of this Glossary shall be understood in accordance with applicable law and, supplementarily, with reasonable customs and practices of the music industry.
