node-mesh phone concept

Concept renders for an articulated node-mesh device: a rigid core flanked by small tiles that fold and re-form around spherical nodes. Story order, top to bottom.

01 — the device

Overall concept: how the mesh opens, folds, and stands.

02 — the marble and the book covers

Cutaway: two tiles ride one ball like covers on a marble; nothing touches the sphere.

03 — the crossing test

Why a ball instead of a hinge: a cylinder jams when fold lines cross, a sphere doesn’t.

04 — captured, not attached

Four tiles share one node through slotted spherical shells that slide over the ball.

05 — passive vs. active

Two architectures compared: captured ball (passive) vs. strut hub with EM locking (active).

06 — the fluid-film node

Evolution: a pressurized micro-film bearing — the shell floats, wear goes to zero, EM valves shape-lock.

07 — node close-up + mesh docking

Single node mechanics, then where the second and third nodes attach in the mesh.
